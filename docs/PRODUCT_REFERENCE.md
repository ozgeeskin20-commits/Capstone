# Product Reference

[EVCify](https://evcify.com/) is a high-level product benchmark for exploring EV charging market analytics, charging infrastructure distribution, province and district analysis, AC/DC statistics, installed capacity analysis, operator comparison, data visualization and decision support. These are benchmark areas for consideration, not claims that every capability is provided by EVCify or implemented in Capstone.

Capstone is an independently developed project. EVCify's intellectual property, branding, assets and proprietary materials remain the property of their respective owners. Do not copy or reproduce its source code, proprietary designs, text or visual assets.
