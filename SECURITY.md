# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please avoid publicly disclosing sensitive vulnerabilities, exploit details or credentials before the project has had an opportunity to investigate and address them. A private vulnerability reporting channel has not been confirmed and needs to be configured. Do not submit sensitive reports through a public issue while that channel is unavailable.

Once a private channel is configured, reports should provide enough information to reproduce the issue, its potential impact and any relevant affected version, without exposing unrelated private data. The maintainers should acknowledge and investigate reports, coordinate remediation and discuss disclosure timing with the reporter.

## Supported versions

No releases exist yet; supported versions have not been established.
